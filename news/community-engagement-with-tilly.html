Supporting Community Well-Being Through Meaningful Experience

Our recent partnership with HFR Solutions CIC and their community canal boat, Tilly, exemplifies this mission by offering individuals from disadvantaged backgrounds the chance to enjoy a unique and uplifting experience.

The Initiative: Sponsoring a Tilly Canal Boat Trip

On July 12th, 2024, we were proud to sponsor and participate in a Men in Sheds trip aboard Tilly. This initiative provides a safe and welcoming environment where individuals can enjoy meaningful conversations, create lasting memories, and strengthen their sense of community — all at no cost to charities and non-profit organizations.

Our Involvement

Ed and Craig from Holistic Fire Safety joined the trip, fully immersing themselves in the experience alongside the attendees. The day was filled with camaraderie, relaxation, and engagement, with our team even being encouraged to take the helm next time!

Impact & Lasting Commitment

This trip reinforced the importance of social connection and the role it plays in community resilience. One of Tilly’s guests summed it up perfectly:

“We had a fantastic time onboard and we will be back again!!!”

Holistic Fire Safety remains committed to strengthening communities, and we look forward to supporting more initiatives that promote connection, inclusion, and positive experiences for all.
