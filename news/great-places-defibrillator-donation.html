News · June 2025

Holistic Fire Safety working with our partners Great Places, proudly donated a defibrillator and cabinet to the Radcliffe Centre of Development

Holistic Fire Safety working with Great Places delivering life saving equipment within the community.

Objectives

We’re proud to be working alongside our partners to support the local community. When we were asked if we could assist the Radcliffe Centre of Development and the surrounding area by donating a defibrillator and cabinet, we jumped at the opportunity.

Having accessible, life-saving equipment in key community spaces can make all the difference in an emergency.

At Holistic Fire Safety, we’re committed to making a real difference through our social value commitments, and we look forward to continuing our support of Great Places on future community-focused projects.

Our Contribution

This is one of two defibrillators we have delivered recently, in partnership with Great Places. The Pat Midgley Community Centre and the Radcliffe Centre of Development now each have life-saving equipment available for the local community to access in an emergency.

A Lasting Partnership

  • Defibrillators save lives — having them accessible in our local community can mean the difference between life and death during a cardiac emergency.
  • Every minute without defibrillation reduces the chance of survival by 10%, which makes fast access absolutely vital.
  • Community defibs empower bystanders to act quickly and confidently while waiting for emergency services to arrive.
  • By placing defibrillators in public spaces, we’re not just preparing for emergencies — we’re protecting our neighbours, friends, and families.

A well-placed defib is a silent guardian that could one day save someone’s parent, child, or colleague.
