Holistic Fire Safety & Lincolnshire Housing Partnership (LHP) community project

Supporting local food banks on the Nunsthorpe Estate in Grimsby.

Objectives

At Holistic Fire Safety, our mission extends beyond fire prevention — we aim to make a meaningful impact in the communities we serve. By reinvesting our social contributions locally, we strive to support those in need while reinforcing safety and well-being in partnership with key stakeholders.

The Initiative

As the festive season approached, we were proud to collaborate with Lincolnshire Housing Partnership (LHP) to support their local food banks, particularly on the Nunsthorpe Estate in Grimsby. Recognising the challenges many families face during winter, we sought to contribute in a way that would provide both immediate relief and long-term value.

Our Contribution

Holistic Fire Safety’s team, including Sam and Mike, personally delivered a range of essential supplies to LHP’s community initiatives. Our donation included:

  • Food essentials for individuals and families
  • Pet food to support households with beloved animals
  • Toiletries and hygiene products to promote well-being
  • Cleaning supplies to encourage safe and sanitary living conditions

A Lasting Partnership

The donation was warmly received by Dave and Emma from LHP, who expressed gratitude for our support. More importantly, this initiative marks the beginning of an ongoing collaboration to enhance community safety, resilience, and well-being. By combining fire safety awareness with tangible social investment, we look forward to building a safer, stronger future for local residents.
